---
name: Calendar Report text treatment
description: User-approved typography, wrapping, note-label, and event-separation rules for exported calendar reports.
---

Calendar Report PDF and Word output uses 11-point black text throughout. Customer names and notes must wrap in full without truncation. Dispatcher and block notes render as content only, without “Dispatcher Notes:” or “Notes:” prefixes. Separate same-day events with distinct bordered boxes and spacing. A Custom Block uses its entered title in the event box; when untitled, use the neutral fallback “Block” rather than displaying the word “Custom.”

**Why:** Reports must remain readable and make each same-day job or block visually unambiguous without hiding customer or note content.

**How to apply:** Keep PDF, emailed-PDF, and Word renderers aligned. Within a day, place Custom Blocks after other events so Potential Jobs always appear above Custom Blocks.
